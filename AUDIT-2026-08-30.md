# Katitos — Language course audit

**Date:** 2026-08-30 · **Method:** direct read of `src/features/language` and the kernel it uses; three parallel exploration sweeps; then a read-only adversarial workflow of 77 agents — a hostile verifier per finding group (54 findings → 34 confirmed, 19 corrected, 1 refuted), six blind bug hunters checked for novelty and attacked by two refuters each (23 survived), a five-lens product panel judged into 20 ranked improvements, and a completeness critic (13 gaps, 12 errors in the plan). Every load-bearing claim re-verified in the code.

**Scope:** the language course — teacher side, student side, engine, data layer, UI system, PWA — with the explicit goal of making `/language` the first PC- and tablet-capable surface of a phone-first PWA. The implementation plan lives in the same document; IDs (B/A/M/P/N/R) are stable across the report, the commits and the workflow verdicts.

---

## Context

`src/features/language` (41 files, 7 580 LOC) is the largest feature in Katitos
and the only one with a _professional_ user. Anastasia teaches languages for a
living; from now on she authors lessons **on a PC** and teaches over a video
call from her phone. Everything in the app was built phone-first — a 32 rem
column with a bottom tab bar — so on a 1440 px screen the lesson builder is a
strip of phone UI floating in the middle of a desk.

The pure logic is genuinely good: `srs.ts`, `exercise-schema.ts`, `pick.ts`
and the grading tests are careful and correct. **The damage is at the seams** —
editor state ↔ refetch, query keys ↔ invalidation, signed URLs ↔ HTTP cache,
service worker ↔ opaque responses — which is exactly what the owner reported:
audio has to be attached twice, modals misbehave, spacing is out of control,
nothing is reusable.

**Owner's constraint (decides the architecture):** the PWA stays a phone app.
`/language` becomes the **first** route family that is PC- and tablet-capable;
the machinery lives in `src/kernel/` so any feature can opt in later with one
hook, without changing how a non-opted route looks. Scope: **full rebuild**,
all four teacher tracks, plus a published audit report.

**Method.** Direct read of the feature and the kernel it uses; three parallel
exploration sweeps (teacher / student + engine / UI system); then a
**read-only adversarial workflow of 77 agents**: one hostile verifier per
finding group (54 findings → 34 confirmed, 19 corrected, 1 refuted), six blind
bug hunters whose finds were checked for novelty against this plan and then
attacked by two independent refuters each (23 survived, 3 killed), a
five-lens product panel judged into a ranked shortlist of 20, and a
completeness critic over the whole (13 gaps, 12 errors in the plan — all
folded in below). Every load-bearing claim was re-verified by me in the code.

## What the adversarial pass changed — read this first

- **B1's root cause was wrong, twice.** My first pass blamed React Query's
  signed-URL cache; the sweep blamed the service worker. The truth: the SW
  **has never cached a single photo or audio clip** — every storage load is a
  cross-origin `<img>`/`<audio>` with no `crossOrigin`, so the response is
  _opaque_ and `sw.ts:137`'s `if (resp.ok)` never stores it. The real stale
  mechanism is the **same signed URL string reused for 59 minutes** × the
  **browser HTTP cache** (`max-age=3600`, the Supabase default because
  `useUpload` sets no `cacheControl`). That is also why the alphabet's
  eviction (`alphabet.ts:56-60`) _does_ work — a new token is a new URL. The
  immutable-path fix survives; the reasoning is corrected (B1, N3, A8).
- **B5 withdrawn.** "New question opens pre-filled" is false: `{editing && …}`
  (`build.route.tsx:305-312`) unmounts the editor on every close. I misread it.
- **B9 downgraded.** React 19 batches the `setKb(0)`/`setKb(kb)` pair; there
  is no per-keystroke bounce. What remains is listener/timer churn on the five
  route-rendered sheets. "Modals misbehave" is re-triaged below (§ Modals).
- **M14 consequence corrected.** `set_block_vocab` is one plpgsql function, so
  the duplicate-key failure rolls back — the word list is _not_ lost; she gets
  a raw Postgres toast and is stuck until she taps the duplicate chip off.
- **Softened:** M3 (a double tap does not duplicate under React 19's discrete
  flush; a re-tap after a slow/failed mutation does), M8 (inert), M13 (double
  push needs a reopen inside the invalidation window), M17 (`attachFor` half
  unreachable), M20 (a global 30 s `staleTime` exists; the complaint is
  unbounded `select('*')`), M21 (a per-lesson "to mark" badge exists in the
  course view; missing is cross-course/home), M22 (lessons _can_ be renamed),
  M26 (latent, unreachable from the UI). Counts corrected in A2, P1, P2.
- **Two sweep claims dropped:** "the page scrolls under the backdrop" (main is
  not a portal ancestor) and "10 px top-bar misalignment" (by design).
- **Nineteen bugs nobody had** (N-series below), five of them blockers — the
  largest: a silent-corruption bug in the exercise editor (N1), a forced
  reload that will wipe her typing on every deploy of this rebuild (N2), and a
  privacy hole on the shared PC (N4).
- **Twelve corrections to the plan itself**, the important ones: nine
  confirmed Majors had no phase; B10 needs a data _repair_, not just a writer
  fix; B4's undo could not undo the one delete that matters; the desk design
  contradicted the design bible without saying so; Phase 1 was too big and
  Phase 9 gated data-loss fixes behind three refactor phases; the shell-cap
  design would not have worked. All fixed below.

## Findings

IDs are stable for traceability (report, commits, workflow verdicts).
Severity follows `AUDIT.md`. "N" = found by the blind hunters and survived
two refuters.

### 🔴 Blockers

**B1 · Audio has to be attached twice.** Root cause: audio paths are
**deterministic** — `storagePaths.languageAudio(id, ext)` → `<vocabId>.webm`
(`kernel/storage/buckets.ts:29`; `api/vocab.ts:126,175`; `api/alphabet.ts:41`)
— uploaded with `upsert: true` (`use-upload.ts:28`) and **no `cacheControl`**
(`:25-32`, so Supabase stamps `max-age=3600`); meanwhile React Query holds the
**same signed URL string for 59 min** (`use-signed-url.ts:15`), the batched
`['signed-urls', …]` key likewise, and a **module-level memo** `signed`
(`use-signed-urls.ts:32`, `peekSignedUrl`) is never purged on write. Same URL

- `max-age=3600` ⇒ the browser HTTP cache returns the old body; the second
  attempt "works" once something rotates. `useUpdateVocab` (`vocab.ts:174-188`)
  evicts nothing; `useRecordLetter` (`alphabet.ts:56-60`) does — and works.
  Exercise audio uses `exercise/${nanoid(10)}` (`exercise-editor.tsx:170`) and
  is immune. App-wide: 22 upload call sites (13 direct, 9 via `usePhotoUpload`),
  8 already evict, **11 `storagePaths.*` builders are deterministic** — every
  re-upload of an avatar, flower, proof photo… is stale for an hour too.

**B2 · `useAddVocab` silently throws the recording away.** `vocab.ts:96-102`
returns the existing id on a duplicate **before** the upload at `:123` —
audio, translation, notes, tags all dropped, reported as success. Row is
inserted before the upload (`:104-135`) so a failed upload leaves a row that
"add" can never repair; `.ilike(termLang, term)` is unescaped (`%`/`_`); the
dedupe select's `error` is ignored.

**B3 · The recorder shows the previous clip after a save.** `reset()` exists
(`use-audio-recorder.ts:184-188`, wired to the trash button) but **the parent
cannot call it** — `AudioRecorder` takes only `{ onRecorded, className }`
(`audio-recorder.tsx:19-25`), no ref, no `resetKey`. `vocab-picker-sheet.tsx:95`
and `alphabet.route.tsx:122` clear their own copy while the sheet stays open,
so the next word is saved silent unless she notices and re-records.

**B4 · One tap destroys work — no confirm, no undo anywhere in the feature.**

| Where                          | What one tap destroys                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `dictionary.route.tsx:147`     | A word, its recording (removed from the bucket, `vocab.ts:198-207`), every `lang_block_vocab` link and **both people's SRS history** (cascade, `20260818000004:44-54`) |
| `build.route.tsx:181`          | A block and everything in it                                                                                                                                           |
| `build.route.tsx:211`          | A question, and by cascade every attempt against it                                                                                                                    |
| `media-block-editor.tsx:88-94` | The uploaded file itself                                                                                                                                               |
| `exercise-editor.tsx:264-270`  | An option — leaving its id in `correct` (M12)                                                                                                                          |

`toast(msg, { action })` (`kernel/ui/toast.ts:92`) is a ready undo channel
used only by the album; 13 other features fall back to `window.confirm()`.
**A snapshot re-insert cannot undo the word delete** — new id, cascaded rows
gone, blob gone — so words need soft delete (§ Data).

**B5 · withdrawn** (refuted — see above). Hygiene only: a `key` on the editor.

**B6 · Multi-word study cards cannot be answered.** `study.route.tsx:220` makes
the answer `<Input>` `readOnly`; `LetterKeys` has **no space and no ё**
(`letter-keys.tsx:13-18`); `answerMatches` refuses missing word boundaries
(`answer-match.ts:16-24`); `modeFor` skips typing only above 24 chars
(`:283`). Every phrase ≤ 24 chars with a space is ungradeable — `lang_vocab`
was backfilled from `phrases`. On a PC he also cannot type at all.

**B7 · A re-check wipes her mark.** `markOne` (`lesson.route.tsx:113-117`)
upserts `status: in_progress, score` over a `graded` row; RLS
(`members_all`) permits — the migration's own comment admits it. Her score
and "Marked · N%" vanish; `graded_at` goes stale; her marking form re-seeds
from the wiped row on next open (`mark.route.tsx:75-79`).

**B8 · Every Cyrillic character renders in a fallback font.** `main.tsx:6-18`
imports 13 `latin-*` subset files of Manrope and Cormorant; `:23-24` imports
`caveat/cyrillic-*` ("her captions are in Russian") — the lesson was learned
for the handwriting face only. Worse than it looks: the subset files carry
**no `unicode-range`** (two identical descriptors, order-dependent), and
**U+0301 — the combining acute the app stores in `lang_vocab.stress` and
announced to her ("write спаси́бо once, with the accent key") — lives only in
the Cyrillic range.** Fix: import the aggregate `@fontsource/<face>/<weight>.css`
files, which declare every subset with `unicode-range` at no extra cost.

**B10 · The two-way product is broken in the writers _and_ the reader.**
Writers: `build.route.tsx:540-546` saves the gloss to `body_es` if
`support === 'es'` **else `body_en`** — in a Spanish course (`supportLangs('es','ru')`
→ `['ru','en']`) her Russian explanation is filed as English; the first
textarea always writes `body_ru` "По-русски…" (`:530-533`); `exercise-editor.tsx:188-190`
can never write `prompt_ru` and its seed (`:91-95`) copies `prompt_ru` into
`prompt_en` on every open-and-save; options are `o.ru` only (`:253-262`);
`table-block.ts:31-33` never emits `{ru}`. Reader: `block-view.tsx:47-52`
renders `body_ru` as the headline and `pick()` as the gloss — in a Spanish
lesson read in Russian, `body` _is_ `body_ru`, the headline test fails, and
**the Spanish sentence is never shown**. **Existing rows are already
corrupted** and need a repair, not just a fix.

**N1 · Re-editing a "Put in order" question makes the jumble the answer.**
`exercise-editor.tsx:100` seeds the sentence box from `payload.tokens`, but
`:146` deliberately stores the **scrambled** pool there. Reopen + Save with no
change ⇒ answer becomes the jumble, pool re-scrambles; every later open
corrupts again; he is marked wrong for the right sentence. Verified with
`scrambleTokens` in node.

**N2 · The app force-reloads mid-session, wiping unsaved authoring.**
`auto-update.tsx:26-38` fires the first time `state` becomes `'stale'`, and
`use-build-status.ts:53-61` re-checks on every `visibilitychange` — the
docstring says "Only on launch" and nothing implements it. Switch to the
camera app, come back after a deploy → `window.location.reload()`. The block
editor saves on blur only (A9). **This rebuild is ~10 deploys; this must land
first.**

**N3 · The service worker's photo/audio cache has never held a byte.** Every
storage load is `new Image()`/`<img>`/`el.src` with **no `crossOrigin`**
(zero hits in `src/`), so `fetch(req)` in `sw.ts:130-141` resolves opaque
(`status 0`, `ok false`) and `storePhoto` (`:137`) never runs. `CacheWarmer`,
`usePrefetchImages`, `IMG_CACHE_MAX` and every "warmed photos survive
offline" comment are dead. Offline photos never worked. Fix: `crossOrigin =
'anonymous'` on the loaders (Supabase sends `ACAO: *`) — which then makes the
SW's token-agnostic key _live_, so the SW eviction in `useUpload` becomes
necessary at the same moment.

**N4 · Sign-out clears nothing — the next person on the shared PC is painted
the previous user's data.** `signOut` is only `supabase.auth.signOut()`
(`auth-provider.tsx:108-110`): no `queryClient.clear()`, the persist key
`katitos:rq-cache:v4` is not namespaced by user and rehydrates
unconditionally for 24 h (`persist.ts:23,33-51`) — including rows RLS would
never hand the other account (hidden wishlist gifts). A PC she and he might
both sign into is the premise of this plan.

**N5 · Paused offline mutations are persisted, then permanently
un-resumable.** TanStack's default `shouldDehydrateMutation` is
`m => m.state.isPaused`; `persist.ts:60-73` overrides only the query filter,
so an offline hand-in is snapshotted, rehydrated without a `mutationFn` (no
`setMutationDefaults` anywhere), and rejected on resume. Set it to
`() => false` until a real outbox exists.

### 🟠 Architectural

**A1 · Mobile-only.** 8 responsive breakpoint classes in _all_ of `src/`
markup, all in this feature (`build.route.tsx:152,227`,
`dictionary.route.tsx:117`); one layout media query (`index.css:144-148`)
widens `--app-max-width` 32→72 rem via `useWideLayout()` — and because the
shell (`app-shell.tsx:121`), every `Sheet` (`sheet.tsx:86`), the toaster and
the login all read that **same global variable**, everything stretches
together. At 900 px `md:` lifts the cap but `lg:` does not split → an 872 px
column of full-width textareas. `/language/mark` is stuck at 512 px.

**A2 · The feature's own controls do not respond to a mouse or keyboard.**
The **36 raw `<button>`s** written inside `features/language` have zero
hover / cursor / focus-visible affordances (the 41 kernel `Button`/`Segmented`
instances do have hover, and a focus ring — blue, see M18). Zero `onKeyDown`,
zero `<form>`, zero `autoFocus` on an answer field. `cursor: pointer` exists
only in `index.css:208`, `scavenger.css:29` and `photo-book.css`.

**A3 · She cannot copy a Russian word out of her own lesson.** `user-select:
none` on `body` (`index.css:7-11`) and on `*` (`:156-160`); only
`input, textarea, [contenteditable='true']` are exempt.

**A4 · `Sheet` is the only overlay primitive, and it is a bottom sheet.**
51 usages / 44 files (9 here); 8 other bespoke full-screen overlays hand-manage
z-index (50/60/80/90/95/101). Defects: no focus trap, no focus move-in or
restore, no `inert`, no `aria-labelledby`, backdrop is a full-screen
`<button>` and the first Tab stop (`:76-81`), Escape is a global listener
(stacked sheets both close), no exit animation (`:67`), `vh` not `dvh`,
safe-area dropped while the keyboard is up (`:119-121`), permanent
`will-change`, always `items-end` (a 72 rem slab welded to the bottom of a
desktop), and the keyboard heuristic (`:44-49`) has **no `visualViewport.scale`
guard** — the moment pinch-zoom is allowed (M23) a 2× zoom reads as a 500 px
keyboard. The `body.style.overflow` lock (`:40`) is dead but harmless.

**A5 · Questions cannot be interleaved with the lesson — the database already
allows it.** `lang_exercises.block_id` exists, `useSaveExercise` accepts
`blockId` (`lessons.mutations.ts:274`), nothing passes it, `useLesson` never
groups by it. No migration needed. (Note the schema's own comment "a ninth
kind is a client change" is **false** — `kind` is a CHECK over eight values
(`20260818000005:23-25`), so new exercise shapes ship as `kind: 'choice'` +
`payload.variant`, not a new kind.)

**A6 · Nothing is optimistic; reorder and create race.** Each blur →
`useUpdateBlock` → invalidate → `useLesson` re-runs three round-trips
(`lessons.queries.ts:51-92`). Reorder = N unbatched `UPDATE`s
(`lessons.mutations.ts:258-264`) computed from stale `blocks`
(`build.route.tsx:122-128`); a double-tap on "text" creates two blocks at the
same `position` (`:241`, no `disabled`, no sort tiebreaker).

**A7 · Query-key prefix collisions** (`kernel/query/keys.ts:132-141`):
`lesson(id)` ⊂ `attempts/blocks/exercises(id)`; `vocab()` ⊂
`vocabReviews()`. The album keys document exactly this trap (`:150-155`).
Every builder mutation wipes his attempts cache; adding one word refetches
every SRS row for both people.

**A8 · Offline: paused, then lost.** With `networkMode` unset a mutation
fired offline is **paused** (not failed) and resumes if the tab survives to
reconnect; it is lost on reload (N5). The UI advances regardless
(`study.route.tsx:76-89`, `lesson.route.tsx:127-146` toasts "Handed in").
`retry: 0` (`query-client.ts:12-14`) makes an online-but-failing write die on
the first attempt with a toast while the score stays on screen. Audio offline:
nothing is cached (N3), signing is a POST (`sw.ts:90`), signed URLs are not
persisted (`persist.ts:72`), `PlayButton` renders `null` (`audio-player.tsx:85`).

**A9 · The builder loses edits.** `BlockEditor` seeds local state once
(`build.route.tsx:358-364`) and never re-syncs; saves on blur only; no
`useTableSync` in `build.route` → last-blur-wins across devices; the language
switch remounts every editor (`key={…:${support}}`, `:167`), discarding
unblurred text. N13 is the same defect in the settings sheet.

**A10 · `answerMatches` has no Unicode normalisation** (`answer-match.ts:30-42`):
NFD `está` ≠ NFC `está`; NFD `мой` → `мои`. `strictYo` is tested and never
passed by any caller. Note: once fixed, Spanish accents are graded strictly —
a red X needs a _reason_ (improvement #12).

**A11 · No teacher role.** The student sees "Edit this lesson"
(`lesson.route.tsx:61-70`); RLS is `is_member()` everywhere; drafts are
hidden client-side. The role is derivable (`course.target_lang === native`,
`courses.route.tsx:88-93` already splits on it) — **but it flickers on every
cold boot** (M24), so gating must wait for `useMembers` to resolve.

## Architecture

### 1 · Storage & audio, done once (kernel)

- **Immutable paths.** `storagePaths.languageAudio` callers pass a per-upload
  id (`${vocabId}-${nanoid(6)}`); `useUpdateVocab` / `useRecordLetter` /
  `useAddVocab` upload the new path, update the row, then remove the old
  object (M27 too). A path never changes content → a new signed URL every
  time → the HTTP cache is defeated by construction. `useUpload` sets
  `cacheControl` explicitly (`'31536000'` for immutable paths).
- **Eviction in `useUpload`** for the 11 legacy deterministic builders: after
  a successful upload, `removeQueries(['signed-url', bucket, path])` + the
  `thumbs/` proxy, `invalidateQueries(['signed-urls', bucket])`, a new
  exported `forgetSignedUrl(bucket, path)` for the module memo — **and**,
  once N3 makes the SW cache real, `caches.keys()` → every `katitos-img-*` →
  `cache.delete(signUrl, { ignoreSearch: true })` for both `object/sign` and
  `render/image/sign` forms (URL built from `import.meta.env.VITE_SUPABASE_URL`).
- **`crossOrigin = 'anonymous'`** on `CacheWarmer`, `usePrefetchImages`, the
  shared `Audio` element and stored `<img>`s (N3). Ships in the same release
  as the eviction above, never before it.
- **`useSignedUrl` gains `refetchInterval`** mirroring `useSignedUrls` (N16).
- **Recorder**: `CANDIDATES` prefers `audio/mp4` (R1); `start()` gets a
  re-entrancy guard (N15); `AudioRecorder` gets a `resetKey` prop (B3).
- **Offline audio** (Phase 10, after N3): when offline, `useSignedUrl`
  returns a synthetic `…/object/sign/<bucket>/<path>?token=offline` after a
  main-thread `caches.match(url, { ignoreSearch: true })` confirms the bytes
  exist — the SW's `ignoreSearch` makes any token work. Later: split audio
  into `katitos-audio-v1` with its own cap (SW change, applies next launch).

### 2 · The opt-in desk layer (kernel)

`src/kernel/ui/desk/`: `useDesk()` stamps `data-desk` and sets a context
flag; `<Desk>` / `<Desk.Rail>` / `<Desk.Canvas>` / `<Desk.Inspector>`, each its
own `min-h-0 overflow-y-auto` scroller. **The cap:** a _second_ token —
`--shell-max-width: var(--app-max-width)` by default, `none` under
`:root[data-desk]` at `≥md` — read only by the shell wrapper
(`app-shell.tsx:121` → `max-w-shell`). Sheets, toaster and login keep reading
`--app-max-width` (32 rem) untouched, so non-desk routes render
byte-identical; on a desk the `bg-surface` panel fills the window, which is
the intent. Below `md:` Rail and Inspector become `Dialog`s and the Canvas is
the page — phone unchanged by construction. `md:`–`lg:` (768–1024): two
panes, inspector as a toggleable drawer — closes the 900 px hole. `lg:`+:
three panes. `app-shell.tsx` swaps `<BottomNav>` for `<SideRail>` only when
`desk && ≥md` (a new `useMediaQuery` in `src/kernel/hooks/`). **Retire**
`useWideLayout` / `data-wide` / `index.css:141-148` and update the three e2e
specs that assert them (`tests/e2e/wide-layout.spec.ts`, `language.spec.ts`,
`lesson-blocks.spec.ts`); add desktop viewport projects to `playwright.config.ts`.

### 3 · `Dialog`, and `Sheet` rebuilt on top of it (kernel)

`dialog.tsx`: portal + manual focus trap (not native `<dialog>` —
`showModal()` fights the `visualViewport` keyboard handling `Sheet` already
solves). Focus trap + restore, `inert` on the app root, `aria-labelledby`,
**stable callback refs** (B9, fixed here once), a module-level
reference-counted scroll lock, Escape scoped to the top of the stack, `dvh`,
a **`visualViewport.scale > 1.01` bail-out** in the keyboard heuristic (#20),
exit animation (`closing` + `animationend`), `placement="bottom" | "center"`
(bottom under `md:`, centre above), `size="auto" | sm | md | lg | full`.
**`Sheet` keeps its exact API as a thin wrapper** → all 51 call sites inherit
the fixes with zero changes. `ConfirmDialog` + `useConfirm()` replace the 13
`window.confirm`s.

### 4 · The interaction layer (kernel)

`index.css`: `button:not(:disabled), [role=button], a[href], summary {

`src/kernel/hooks/use-hotkeys.ts` (window-level, ignores fields unless

### 6 · Data

- **Keys** (A7): `attempts(id)` → `['lang','attempts',id]`; delete the unused

  key moves with the shape.
- **Delete policy**: snapshot-undo (re-insert within the 9 s `toast({action})`)
  for blocks, questions and options; **soft delete** (`deleted_at`) for words

  `ConfirmDialog` only for course/unit delete and for publishing.

  the old bundle for one session):
  1. _(Phase 1)_ `lang_vocab.deleted_at` + `lang_lessons.deleted_at`; the old
     bundle shows a soft-deleted row for one session — harmless.
  2. _(Phase 1)_ A `before update` trigger on `lang_lesson_progress`: when the
     row is `graded` and the incoming status is a student status, keep
     `status`/`score`/`graded_at` silently (B7) — protects even the old bundle,
     never raises into its error toast.
  3. _(Phase 1, sequenced)_ **B10 data repair**: for courses with
     `target_lang = 'es'`, move Cyrillic `body_en` → `body_ru` where `body_ru`
     is null (guarded by `~ '[А-Яа-яЁё]'`), same for `prompt_en` → `prompt_ru`
     and table headings. Idempotent. **Run one session after the writer fix
     is live on both devices**, or the old bundle re-corrupts it on the next
     blur.
  4. _(Phase 7)_ `lang_attempts.teacher_note text` + `teacher_score real`.
  5. _(Phase 7)_ `lang_lesson_progress.status` CHECK admits `'returned'`.
  6. _(Phase 10, optional)_ `lang_couple_days` for the shared streak (#14) —
     or derive it from existing rows and skip the table.
- **Exercise variants**: new shapes (stress placement, minimal pairs,
  word-bank cloze, listen-then-choose) ship as existing `kind`s +
  `payload.variant` — the `kind` CHECK makes a ninth kind a migration.
- **Sign-out** (N4): `queryClient.clear()`, remove the persist key, namespace
  the key by `session.user.id` and refuse a snapshot from another user, post
  `caches.delete` to the SW — on `SIGNED_OUT` and on the dev switcher.
- **Auto-update** (N2): apply only inside a launch window (`bootAt` +
  ~10 s); otherwise leave it to the Settings row; a `beforeunload` dirty guard
  as the second belt.
- **Offline** (Phase 10): `networkMode: 'offlineFirst'` for language mutations;
  `shouldDehydrateMutation` re-enabled only once `setMutationDefaults`
  registers every language mutation and boot calls `resumePausedMutations()`;
  `gcTime` raised to the snapshot lifetime (N17). **Scope honestly:** the
  outbox cannot carry blobs (`JSON.stringify` flattens a `Blob`; `upload()`
  is not a mutation) — recordings require a connection and the recorder says
  so; answers, grades and progress queue.
